Build up your Snowmentum, destroy everything in your path, and leave snow-body unscathed!  In Snowmentum, play as a snowball as you roll down a mountain slope and destroy as much of the environment as you can.  As your snowball grows, previously impassable obstacles become easily crushed.  Destroy objects to gain points, and rack up as high a score as you can!

Client Work

This project was made as part of a contract with the 8-Bit Beercade of Peoria, and is currently available to the public.

Working with a client introduced new challenges for us to overcome. We had to learn how to make clear concept documents that communicated our vision for the game to the client, work under platform restrictions (in our case, the arcade machine), and adapt to client feedback.

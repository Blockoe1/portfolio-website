The Games have begun! The gods have selected four players as their champions to enter the Minotaur's Labyrinth and gather treasure on their behalf.

In this four-player maze runner, you have 90 seconds to traverse the labyrinth collecting treasure, stealing from your opponents, and avoiding the Minotaur’s pursuit. Collect treasure for the gods, and at the end of FUSE, the god with the most treasure collected will be crowned the winner!

At FUSE

This game was presented as a 90 second experience at FUSE 2026, with the premise that it would be a continuous experience across the entire day as players could come back later and see how their god was doing on the leaderboard.

For those curious, Athena was our winner for day 1 and Poseidon was our winner for day 2.

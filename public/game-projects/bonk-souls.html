Project Background

This project was made for a summer class designed to introduce students to Unreal Engine. Although I had already worked in Unreal before, this was a chance to expand my skillset within the tool. I challenged myself to scope higher than I normally would by making a souslike, and I specifically focused on trying to learn Unreal's animation tools.

Why a souslike? I've never been a soulslike player, but right before the class started, I finished my first playthrough of Elden Ring and was fascinated by the experience. This project was my opportunity to dive deep into the design semantics that make souslikes so satisfying by making a mini-one myself.

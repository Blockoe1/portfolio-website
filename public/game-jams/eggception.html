What is eggception but an egg within an egg within an egg...?

In this boss rush, you must fight your way out and eggscape from the confines of the outermost shell. Along the way, you will face great dangers. Your brain may become scrambled. Your spirit may crack under the pressure. But, if you eggscape, you will find a hidden truth.

Individual Contributions:

  • Programmed the boss systems, including a state machine based boss-making tool, allowing designers to easily change boss behaviors.
  • Collaborated with our lead designer to implement the first boss, the Mixing Bowl, based on their design specifics.
  • Utilized ProBuilder to create a screen shatter transition effect for breaking through each egg layer.
  • Added polish effects to player attacks, such as a swing trail and screenshake.
  • Assisted with playtesting and balancing bosses based on playtester feedback.
  • Enhanced player communication by implementing telegraphs for boss attacks.

Created for the 2026 Crossplay Game Jam, a collaborative game jam between Bradley University and 8 other universities across the midwest.


Theme: "You can't make an omlette without breaking a few eggs."


This team is a continuation of TeAm that worked on Carrie in Peril, with all four original members returning, and the team expanding to include many new friends that are also Bradley University Teaching Assistants.

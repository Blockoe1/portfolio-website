Flocket Ship

Send flightless birds to the moon in this short, high-pressure time management game.

Itch.io

12

4 Days

Programmer, Designer

Unity

Welcome your favorite flightless birds into space! You have 3 minutes to launch your spaceship but your crew is falling apart! Build your rocket and guide your crew through the most intense T-minus sequence in the Antarctic!

As you progress through the game, you have to make sure your crew do not panic, the rocket is safe, engineer to the audience liking, and more!

Jam Details

Created for the 2026 GMTK Game Jam.


Theme: "Countdown"


The game was rated in the top 10% on average out of over 10k entries.

Our team was made up of 12 Bradley students working together online over the summer. Working online as opposed to in-person as we were all used to introduced additional challenges, especially in communication and task organization. It taught me a lot about team communication and stepping up in part to help organize our programmers.

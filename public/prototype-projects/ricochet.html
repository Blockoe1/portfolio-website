Ricochet is a stealth-based first-person shooter centered around reflecting bullets. Make your way through three puzzling levels full of patrolling robotic enemies using the unique properties of light.

Make strategic use of mirrors throughout the facility to reflect the bullets of your trusty laser pistol and take out threats covertly. Direct confrontation is... not advised.
